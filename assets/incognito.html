You're in an incognito tab

Pages you view here don't share cookies or site data with your regular tabs, and that data is discarded as soon as this tab closes — SCIFI opens incognito tabs with their own private session rather than just hiding them from the tab list.

This doesn't make you anonymous to sites you sign into, your network, or MITOS itself. SCIFI doesn't keep browsing history for any tab yet, incognito or not.
